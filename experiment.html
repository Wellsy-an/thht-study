Tangram Puzzle Task: Instructions七巧板任务说明

What is a tangram puzzle?什么是七巧板题？

A tangram is a classic puzzle made of seven flat geometric pieces: five triangles, one square and one parallelogram. To solve a tangram puzzle, a person arranges all seven pieces, without overlapping, to form a given outline shape.七巧板是一种经典拼图，由七块平面几何图形组成：五个三角形、一个正方形和一个平行四边形。解答七巧板题时，需要将全部七块拼板不重叠地拼成指定的轮廓图形。

Your task您的任务

In the next task, you will select 11 tangram puzzles for another participant enrolled in this study. You will not solve any puzzles yourself; this task does not measure your own puzzle-solving ability.在接下来的任务中，您将为本研究的另一位参与者选择11道七巧板题。您本人不需要解题；此任务并不测量您自己的拼图能力。

Debriefing研究说明

Thank you for taking part in this study. Your time and effort are greatly appreciated. Please read the following information about the study.感谢您参与本研究。我们非常感谢您付出的时间和精力。请阅读以下关于本研究的信息。

The purpose of this study本研究的目的

The real purpose of this study is to examine how different types of AI-generated short-video content influence online decision-making behaviour. Participants watched different AI-generated videos, and we compare the choices made in the tangram puzzle task across these video conditions.本研究的真实目的是考察不同类型的AI生成短视频内容如何影响在线决策行为。参与者观看了不同的AI生成视频，我们将比较不同视频条件下参与者在七巧板任务中的选择。

Why the task was not fully explained为何事先未完全说明任务目的

Before the study, we did not fully explain the purpose of the tangram puzzle task. This was necessary to avoid influencing your natural choices: knowing what we were studying could have changed how you selected the puzzles.在研究开始前，我们没有完全说明七巧板任务的目的。这样做是为了避免影响您的自然选择：如果您事先了解研究内容，可能会改变您选择题目的方式。

No one was affected by your choices您的选择不会影响任何人

No real participant will be harmed by, or lose any payment because of, the puzzles you selected.不会有任何真实参与者因您选择的题目而受到伤害或失去任何报酬。
