Step 5 of 11

Tangram Puzzle Task: Instructions

What is a tangram puzzle?

A tangram is a classic puzzle made of seven flat geometric pieces: five triangles, one square and one parallelogram. To solve a tangram puzzle, a person arranges all seven pieces, without overlapping, to form a given outline shape.

Your task

In the next task, you will select 11 tangram puzzles for another participant enrolled in this study.

  • There are 30 puzzles in total, and they vary in difficulty.
  • The other participant will attempt to solve the 11 puzzles you select.
  • If the other participant completes at least 10 of the 11 puzzles within the time limit, they may receive a small bonus.

Please complete this task carefully and make your selections thoughtfully.

Step 11 of 11

Debriefing

Thank you for taking part in this study. Your time and effort are greatly appreciated. Please read the following information about the study.

The purpose of this study

The real purpose of this study is to examine how different types of AI-generated short-video content influence online decision-making behaviour. Participants watched different AI-generated videos, and we compare the choices made in the tangram puzzle task across these video conditions.

Why the task was not fully explained

Before the study, we did not fully explain the purpose of the tangram puzzle task. This was necessary to avoid influencing your natural choices: knowing what we were studying could have changed how you selected the puzzles.

No one was affected by your choices

No real participant will be harmed by, or lose any payment because of, the puzzles you selected.

If you feel uncomfortable

If any part of this study made you feel uncomfortable or distressed, please contact the researcher at [researcher name, email address], or contact [university counselling / student support service, contact details].

Your data

Now that you know the real purpose of the study, you may decide whether your data are kept. Your decision has no consequences for you. Please choose one option.
